Privacy Policy

How Frenz handles information when you use the app.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

EnglishItaliano

What we collect

Frenz does not require an account and does not send personal data to a server. Player names remain on the device and are used only during a game. Language and onboarding state are stored locally.

Permissions and sharing

Frenz does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or notification access. The current version uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and does not share data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until deleted by the user. To remove it, uninstall Frenz.

Changes

If our data practices or features change, we will update this notice. For privacy questions, use the support information on the Frenz App Store page.
